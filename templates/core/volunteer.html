Lend your Skills. Multiply your Impact.

Volunteer with us and help empower the next generation of African entrepreneurs by contributing your time, skills, and experience.

Where you fit

There are meaningful opportunities to support our mission.

Whether your expertise is in programme coordination, event management, marketing and communications, content creation, technology, design, administration, or community engagement, there are meaningful opportunities to support our mission.

As a volunteer, you'll become part of a passionate community committed to equipping young entrepreneurs with the knowledge, networks, and opportunities they need to build successful businesses.

Every contribution, whether behind the scenes or directly supporting participants, helps strengthen our programmes and expand our impact across Africa.

Most volunteer opportunities are designed to be flexible and can be carried out remotely, allowing you to contribute from anywhere in the world while making a meaningful difference.
