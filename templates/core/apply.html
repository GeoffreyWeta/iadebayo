Who can apply?

  • Be an undergraduate or a graduate
  • Own or co-own an existing business with measurable traction
  • Operate a business within Africa
  • Demonstrate commitment to growing your venture
  • Have reliable access to a laptop or smartphone and the internet throughout the programme
