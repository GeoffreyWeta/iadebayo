Facilitators

Facilitators deliver practical, interactive sessions that equip participants with the knowledge, frameworks, and tools they need to build and grow successful businesses. Sessions are conducted virtually on weekends and typically last 60 minutes. Depending on your area of expertise, you may facilitate one or two sessions during a cohort.

✳

Mentors

Our mentorship programme is a structured three-month journey designed to provide participants with personalised guidance as they navigate the realities of building a business. Mentors are carefully matched with entrepreneurs based on their industry, experience, and development needs. Mentors typically meet with their mentees two to four times each month. Session schedules are arranged flexibly between mentors and mentees.
