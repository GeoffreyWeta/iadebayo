{% comment %} One horizontal bar list, plus the table view of the same numbers. Params: title, data (from analytics.breakdown / multi_breakdown), note (opt), unit (opt, default "applicants"), ordinal (opt — "yes" draws the one-hue ramp in band order instead of a single hue). Every bar is direct-labelled with its count and share. That is not decoration: it is the relief channel the palette gate requires, and it means the chart is still readable to someone who cannot separate the fill from the track. Bars are scaled to the largest row, not to the total — see analytics._as_bars for why — so the printed % and the bar length answer different questions. The caption says which is which. {% endcomment %}
